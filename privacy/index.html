Who we are

Fusion Farm LLC operates fusionfarm.net, with headquarters in Chicago and offices in Minneapolis and Seattle.

What we collect and why

When you send an inquiry, we receive your name, email, company (if provided), topic and message. We use those details to respond, assess the work and provide requested services. Please do not include passwords, confidential records or sensitive personal information in the form.

Website services

DreamHost hosts this website and processes standard server logs, which may include IP addresses, requested pages and browser information, to operate and secure the site. The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent spam when message delivery is enabled. Turnstile processes technical browser and network signals for security. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising trackers or third-party font services.

How messages are handled

Messages are routed to the Fusion Farm inbox for the selected topic. Hosting, email and spam-prevention providers process information as needed to provide those services. We do not sell personal information. We retain correspondence only as needed for the inquiry, ongoing work, security and applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Cookies and external sites

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Spam protection may use technical browser storage or security mechanisms. Links to portfolio domains take you to separate websites with their own practices.
