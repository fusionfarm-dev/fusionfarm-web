Privacy

Privacy notice.

Short version: we collect only what you send us, use it only to reply, and don't track you.

What we collect

When you use the contact form, we receive the name, company, email address, topic and message you enter. When you email us directly, we receive your message and address in the usual way.

How we use it

Only to reply to you and to carry out any work you ask us to do. We don't sell, rent or share it for marketing, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or social-media trackers. Fonts and images are served from our own domain.

Service providers

Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability. Form messages are delivered to our inbox by a mail-delivery service. If a spam check is shown on the contact form, it is Cloudflare Turnstile, which processes limited technical data to tell people from bots.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to respond and to run any engagement that follows, then delete what we no longer need.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to our client support desk.
