# fusionfarm-web

Public website for Fusion Farm LLC — https://fusionfarm.net

Static site generated by `python3 tools/build.py` (stdlib only) from `src/` into
`public/`. Edit `src/`, never `public/` by hand; rebuild and commit both.
See README for structure, deploy and the contact-form backend.

## Identity (source of truth: LinkedIn kit, fusionfarm-linkedin PAGE-COPY.md / HANDOFF-WEBSITE.md)
- Headline: "Intelligence, engineered."  Eyebrow: "Fusion Farm LLC · Intelligence Systems"
- One-liner: "Fusion Farm designs and builds intelligence systems: the technology that turns
  scattered data, documents and signals into clear, decision-ready answers."
- Supporting line: "Intelligence systems, research and technology solutions for real-world decisions."
- Services, in this order: Intelligence systems · Technology solutions · Research and analysis ·
  Due diligence and assessments · Special projects worldwide
- Process: start with the decision → design the smallest system that answers it → ship into the
  tools you already use, improve from real use. Proof line: "We build what we use. We use what we build."
- Industries (credibility, not the offer; not shown as a section on the site): commercial aviation aftermarket · hospitality & food ·
  commercial real estate · equipment & industrial assets
- Locations: Chicago, Minneapolis, Seattle, in that order, set centered under the wordmark in header
  and footer. No "HQ" label on the site (Chicago stays first; JSON-LD lists all three).

## Language rules
- Use: intelligence systems, systems, data, research, technology solutions, development,
  engineered, decision-ready, "we design and build", "one accountable lead".
- Avoid as identity/headline: "AI-powered", "AI-native", "AI solutions" (AI may appear only as a
  tool inside a service description). Avoid "boots on the ground" / "field-proven" as the lead.
- Not a web design studio: never sell websites or landing pages.
- Never "solo founder" or "freelancer".
- Tone: senior, calm, precise. Business execs who build, never "tech bro".

## Brand system
- Ink `#0B0F14`, panels `#121821`, lines `#223040`, text `#EEF1F4`, body `#C5CDD6`, muted `#8C98A6`
- Jade `#5ED6AA` primary accent; signal blue `#7FB8FF` secondary
- Orbit colors: Systems = jade, Data = blue, Field = white; caption "Experience at the core"
- Wordmark: FUSION FARM in Michroma (0.06em tracking), outlined to SVG by `tools/make_wordmark.py`
  (`assets/img/wordmark.svg` light, `wordmark-ink.svg` dark). Never set the name in live text as a logo.
- Manrope (800 headlines, 500–700 body) and JetBrains Mono uppercase labels (~0.14em), self-hosted
- The atomic mark (`assets/img/mark-white.png`, from the GitHub org avatar) always sits next to
  the wordmark. Do not redraw or recolor it. Lockups for other uses: `brand/fusionfarm-lockup-*.png`.
- Hero map: supplied network image (`assets/img/hero-map.webp`, off-edge arcs painted out) with green
  traced lines (`src/data/hero-lines.json`) and CHI/MSP/SEA office dots drawn live on top.
  The source image is stock: confirm a licence before launch.
- "How we work" background: original server-rack render from `tools/make_rack_bg.py`.
- Motion is subtle (line fade-in, hub pulse) and off under prefers-reduced-motion.

## Content rules
- Only real, confirmed content. No placeholder copy, invented clients, testimonials or metrics.
- No plain-text email addresses or phone numbers in HTML; `site.js` assembles them from
  `data-mail` / `data-addr` / `data-tel`.
- Domain prices and availability come from Pedro; unconfirmed names stay `listed: false`.
- Public desks: research@ (projects, research & engagements), domains@, support@. pete@ is not shown.
- No third-party scripts, fonts or trackers (Turnstile only when enabled).
